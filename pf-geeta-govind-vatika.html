The project

Nineteen acres in Taj Nagri Phase-II, built and fitted out by ADA. The asset is already in place: musical fountains, a 40-minute Krishna Leela laser show, an open-air amphitheatre, a waterbody, eight kiosks, a Tulsi forest of over a hundred species, and thematic sculpture and landscaping across the site. The operator pays a monthly licence fee and retains gate collections.

Why the facility is structured this wayTerm loan against mobilisation, not opex. The term component is ₹0.80 Cr, matched to the capital items in section 02. Operating cost is met from collections, so no part of it is term-funded.

A ₹0.20 Cr revolving limit, not a year of opex. The guarantee fee is charged on the sanctioned limit in year 1 and on the outstanding balance thereafter, so an over-sanctioned limit costs fee on undrawn money and consumes group CGTMSE ceiling. The limit is sized to peak within-year drawdown.

A 1-year principal moratorium. Year 1 covers interest and fee from operations; principal begins in year 2 when EBITDA is ₹0.51 Cr.

Ceiling consumption. ₹1.00 Cr of the group's ₹10 Cr per-borrower CGTMSE ceiling.
Licence fee sensitivityThe model assumes a winning bid of ₹36 L a year, 20% above ADA's ₹30 L reserve. Solving for the licence fee at which the project IRR falls to the 12.10% all-in cost of this facility gives ₹80.9 L a year, or ₹6.74 L a month — about 2.7× the reserve. Above that level the project no longer covers the cost of the debt funding it.

09

Risks and open items

Licence fee at auction
High
The forward e-auction has no cap and the fee escalates 5% a year on whatever is bid. Every ₹1 lakh a year above the modelled ₹36 L costs about ₹8.1 lakh across the seven-year term. The project stops covering the cost of its debt above ₹80.9 L a year.
Show and activity tariffs
Medium–High
ADA fixes the gate at ₹20 and approves every other rate, with ten days' notice required for any revision. The show and activity lines together are 46% of year-3 revenue.
Laser and fountain asset condition
Medium–High
The agency inherits equipment of unknown age and must replace anything reaching end of life at its own cost, with the onus of proving irreparability also on the agency. Not provided for in the cost model.
Footfall in the opening season
Medium
Year 1 assumes 3.75 lakh visits, about 1,027 a day, at a site with no operating history. Year-1 EBITDA is ₹0.17 Cr; a shortfall of roughly 7% in revenue removes it.
No permanent structures
Medium
Clause 3.3.2 bars permanent construction, so the activity layer must be demountable — higher unit cost and shorter asset life. The capex schedule assumes an eight-year life on that equipment.
Electricity arrears
Medium
Electricity is paid direct to the discom monthly. VAPPL carries ₹2.14 Cr of accumulated arrears across the existing estate, which is a condition precedent to any lender sanction.
Concession renewal
Medium
Seven years, extendable by four at ADA's discretion, with the licence fee revisable for the extended term. No extension value is included in any figure in this deck.
Open items
  • 1.The winning licence fee is not known until the e-auction concludes. Every figure in this deck is built on a ₹36 L annual bid and requires re-running against the actual award.
  • 2.The fountain and laser show tariff is not set in the RFP. It is to be agreed with ADA at least ten days before implementation. The model assumes ₹100 in year 1 rising to ₹130 by year 7.
  • 3.Commissioning dates and AMC history for the laser and fountain equipment have not been provided. The cost model carries routine AMC but no provision for end-of-life replacement.
  • 4.Clause 5.1.1(h) of the RFP defines “Park” as Subash Park rather than Geeta Govind Vatika — a drafting carry-over from an earlier ADA tender. Worth a written clarification.
  • 5.Milestone 3 requires the first six months of licence fee within 7 days of the work order, while possession need only be taken within 7 days of executing the agreement. The sequencing should be confirmed.
  • 6.Activity-layer capex assumes ADA approves the design concept submitted within 15 days of the work order. Rejection of material items would affect both the capex and roughly ₹0.79 Cr of year-3 revenue.
