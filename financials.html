Year-on-Year Commentary
FY22-23: Single park (EMV only). Post-COVID operating leverage drove 36% EBITDA and 32.5% net margin — the performance benchmark for the E-O-D model at maturity. Finance costs near-zero.
FY23-24: Revenue +18%. Employee costs +126% as team scaled ahead of multi-park expansion. Deliberate investment year — Trampoline Park and Soft Play added, major EMV renovations. Margin compressed to 13.6%.
FY24-25: Sector-wide down year. Revenue −15%. Wonderla −4.6%, PVR INOX −5%. E-O-D's steeper decline reflects monsoon meeting insufficient covered-activity capacity + simultaneous DME pre-launch. Near-breakeven. PP&E doubled during the year.
FY25-26: 4-park ramp-up year. Revenue all-time high at ₹16.29 Cr (+27%). EMV rebounded with new rides (Go Kart, Laser Tag, Rain Dance upgrade) running their first full season. EAC profitable in 6 months at ₹200. EBITDA 14.5% — structurally strong; FY26-27 is first full year at 4-park scale.

Medium–High

₹400 justified long-term by Phase 2 activities — pre-Phase 2 churn risk.
Medium
₹400 launched 22 May 2026 on existing activities (not raise-dependent). Phase 2 (Jun/Jul 2027) is the retention accelerant. EAC's 30.7% margin at ₹200 suggests existing activities have strong value justification.

Zipline is EMV's top-draw attraction. Internal ride-attributed revenue tracking being formalised. At ₹18 Cr EMV revenue (FY26-27) AMC is 6.2% — improving.
Debt Service
₹7.85 Cr debt + ₹71L finance costs. EBITDA interest coverage 4.7× (FY26).
Medium
Raise earmarks ₹2–3 Cr NBFC repayment — reduces annual finance cost by ~₹30–40L from FY27. Coverage improves to 5×+ by FY28.
Location Dependency
All parks on government concessions. Regulatory / political risk.
Medium
Two separate government bodies (DTTDC + ADA). 4-city presence reduces single-site concentration. Government relationship capital spans DTTDC, ADA, GDA, DDA, BDA, Haryana Tourism, UP Tourism, Swadesh Darshan 2.0.
