# Fundraise deck — figures now overtaken by trading

This note exists because `index.html` and `financials.html` — the deck submitted to Almonds — are
deliberately frozen and are not regenerated by the model. **Nothing here has been applied to them.**
It records which figures management's reported position has moved past, so that reissuing the deck
stays a separate and deliberate decision.

Raised 25 August 2026, from the Subhash Park FY26-27 position reported by management.

## What changed

Subhash Park has booked **₹27 lakh of revenue in FY26-27 to date** — five months, 1 April to end
August 2026, before the season starts. On the current run rate the site is expected to **cross ₹1
crore for the year**.

## What that overtakes in `financials.html`

The park-by-park revenue table carries this row, in ₹ crore:

| ESP · Subhash Park, Agra | FY23-24 | FY24-25 | FY25-26 | FY26-27 | FY27-28 | FY28-29 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| As published | — | — | 0.14 | **~0.50** | **~1.00** | ~1.50–2.00 |

- **FY26-27 at ~₹0.50 Cr is understated by roughly half.** The year is tracking to ₹1 crore or more,
  and ₹27 lakh is already booked against it in the off-season.
- **FY27-28 at ~₹1.00 Cr arrives a year early.** If FY26-27 lands at ₹1 crore, FY27-28 and FY28-29
  need re-basing rather than a simple uplift — the whole curve shifts left.
- The FY25-26 actual of ₹0.14 Cr is unaffected, as is the 17.5% net margin recorded over the first
  four months.

## Knock-on figures worth re-checking before any reissue

Not verified as part of this note — listed because they are computed from, or argued on, the
park-level revenue build:

- Group revenue projections for FY26-27 onward, and the EBITDA and PAT that follow them.
- The 27–29% group EBITDA margin target for FY28-29. A park trading ahead of plan on a zero-capex
  base changes the blend.
- The ₹10 crore raise at a ₹90 crore pre-money. Valuation rests on the forward revenue curve, and one
  of the operating sites is now ahead of it.

## Where this *has* been applied

The project-finance pack (`pf-*.html`, `pptx/`, `pdf/`) cites the trading position rather than the
company model — see section 03 of the Geeta Govind Vatika deck, generated from
`company.profile.subhash_park` in `model/pf_model.json`.

The two documents therefore disagree on Subhash Park's FY26-27 revenue. The project-finance pack is
self-contained and carries no link to the fundraise deck, so a lender reading one never sees the
other — but anyone holding both will see the difference.
